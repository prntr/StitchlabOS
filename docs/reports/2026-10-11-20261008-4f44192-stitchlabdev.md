# First boot of `stitchlabdev` on StitchLabOS 20261008-4f44192

> Dated evidence, 2026-10-11. This report is never corrected afterwards; later
> findings go into a new report. It checks on hardware the beta5 fixes that need
> no physical action beyond the card and one firmware flash: first-boot naming,
> cloud-init, the Wi-Fi scripts, `live_jogd` without a dongle, and the Pico on the
> image's Klipper. Times on the Pi are BST (Europe/London, the image default).

## Result in one paragraph

A fresh card in the machine formerly called `stitchlab07` came up as
`stitchlab-51ad` with the access point `Stitchlab-51ad`, the suffix being the
last four hex digits of the board serial, and kept that name over two reboots.
cloud-init finished `done` without errors (the 2026-09-28 run: `degraded`). The
Wi-Fi status and profile scripts returned valid JSON with profile UUIDs on a
real NetworkManager, `live_jogd` stayed `inactive` without a dongle, and no unit
failed. The Pico ran older firmware; `stitchlab-flash-pico --uart` brought it to
the host's Klipper on the first attempt, but its closing check reported "MCU not
confirmed yet" although the MCU had connected. Renaming the machine to
`stitchlabdev` with `hostnamectl` left `/etc/hosts` and Avahi on the old name
until both were fixed by hand.

## The Pico flash

The Pico ran Klipper `aea1bcf`, the host `v0.13.0-786-g461c4e3722c3`, and
Klipper was `ready` with that mismatch. With the owner's go-ahead and the 24 V
switch in reach, the owner ran
`stitchlab-flash-pico --uart 2>&1 | tee ~/flash-pico-run3.log` over `ssh -t`;
the log is on the machine.

| Step | Log |
|---|---|
| Klipper stopped | `Stopping klipper (it holds /dev/serial0)` |
| Attempt 1/3 | `Detected Klipper binary version v0.13.0-786-g461c4e3722c3, MCU: rp2040`, `Katapult Connected`, `Software Version: ec59b9b` |
| Write | `Write complete: 168 pages`, `Verification Complete: SHA = 18FCD940554D16A98A558FBF86AA2C99AE60AFB2`, `Programming Complete` |
| Closing check | **`MCU not confirmed yet`**, followed by the first lines of a fresh `klippy.log` (`Log rollover at Sun Oct 11 18:45:59 2026`, `Starting Klippy...`) |
| Afterwards | `klippy.log`: `Loaded MCU 'mcu' 143 commands (v0.13.0-786-g461c4e3722c3 / …)`, `Configured MCU 'mcu'`; `/printer/objects/query?mcu`: `mcu_version` `v0.13.0-786-g461c4e3722c3`, `ready` |

The flash worked. The closing check restarts Klipper, waits a fixed 8 s and
looks for the MCU line in the last 200 lines of `klippy.log`; this time the
restart rolled the log over and Klippy connected after the 8 s, so the script
reported a failure that had not happened.

## Open issues and proposed fixes

| # | Issue | Proposed fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The rename in `11-commissioning.md` is incomplete**: `hostnamectl` leaves `/etc/hosts` and Avahi on the old name, and `sudo` warns on every call | A helper `stitchlab-rename <name>` that sets the hostname, rewrites the `127.0.1.1` line and restarts Avahi; the doc points at it |
| 2 | **`stitchlab-flash-pico` reports "MCU not confirmed yet" after a successful flash** when Klippy needs more than 8 s | Poll for Klipper `ready` (Moonraker `/printer/info` or the MCU line) for up to 30 s instead of one look after a fixed wait |
| 3 | The journal keeps only the current boot, so first-boot evidence is gone after the first reboot | Decide whether a commissioning machine should keep a persistent journal (`Storage=persistent`), at least until it is commissioned |
